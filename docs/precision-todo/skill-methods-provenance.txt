=== ATTACK / STRENGTH / DEFENCE  (shared page) ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Melee_training
- "==Fastest Experience==" section headers: "Levels 1-30: Questing" (Waterfall Quest at level 1 -> 30 Att/Str,
  "no requirements, making it the fastest way to train a brand-new account"), "Levels 30 to 50: Crabs"
  (Gemstone Crab/Tlati Rainforest; Ammonite Crab/Fossil Island; Sand Crab/Hosidius/Crabclaw/Avium Savannah),
  "Levels 50-70: Nightmare Zone - Normal Rumble" (AFK w/ absorption+overload, needs 5 quests),
  "Levels 70-99: Nightmare Zone" ("Fastest solo experience per hour for Attack and Strength"; Dharok's
  "becomes optimal at Hitpoints 92+").
- General training notes: aggressive->Strength; "Defence can be trained by using Ancient Magicks on defensive
  or chinchompas on Long fuse against Maniacal monkeys for more experience per hour than using melee."
  Style guidance (accurate/aggressive/defensive/controlled) underpins the DEFENCE Defensive/Controlled tip.

=== HITPOINTS ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Hitpoints_training
- "Each point of damage dealt directly by the player yields 1.33 Hitpoints XP... any combat training method
  that offers a high XP rate will also train Hitpoints." Links to melee/magic/ranged training.
- "Ice Burst/Barrage" section: cast on Skeletal Monkeys / maniacal monkeys at Ape Atoll (70 Magic + Monkey
  Madness). "Chinchompas" section: chin maniacal monkeys for fast Ranged + HP.

=== RANGED ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Ranged_training
- "Levels 1-45: Using dwarf multicannon" (needs Dwarf Cannon; ogres east of Castle Wars ~40k xp/h).
- "Levels 45-99: Chinning maniacal monkeys" ("the fastest way to train Ranged", Kruk's Dungeon/Ape Atoll,
  needs Monkey Madness II chapter II). "Levels 1-45/70: Crabs" AFK cheaper alt; darts recommended weapon.

=== PRAYER ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Prayer_training
- Starting off: quests (The Restless Ghost, Priest in Peril, Recruitment Drive, Holy Grail -> 1..30).
- "Offering bones at a gilded altar" = "The fastest way to train Prayer... 350% experience per bone" (POH,
  both incense burners lit, 75 Con). "offer bones at the Chaos altar in the Wilderness instead... only
  slightly slower but offers twice as much experience per bone."

=== MAGIC ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Magic_training
- "Levels 4-55/99: Enchanting bolts" ("one of the fastest ways to train magic", "very expensive");
  "switch to casting Low Level Alchemy at level 21, as this is much cheaper."
- "Level 55+: High Level Alchemy" ("one of the best training methods for gaining passive Magic experience").
- "Levels 62-99: Bursting/barraging on Ancient Magicks" (needs Desert Treasure I; Slayer tasks - dust devils,
  Greater Nechryaels - "one of the most efficient ways to train Slayer and Magic").

=== RUNECRAFT ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Runecraft_training
- "Levels 1-32/34: Questing" (Client of Kourend etc.). "Levels 27-99: Guardians of the Rift".
- "Levels 23-99: Lava runes" ("the fastest way to train Runecraft when not using essence runners", Fire Altar,
  earth runes, binding necklace). "Levels 77-99: Crafting blood/soul runes in Arceuus".

=== CONSTRUCTION ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Construction_training
- "Levels 1-33: Starting off" (crude chair -> wooden bookcase -> larder -> oak tables; saw+hammer, steel nails).
- "Levels 33-52/74: Oak larders" (Kitchen, 8 oak planks, 480 xp; oak dungeon doors at 74).
- "Levels 52-99: Mahogany furniture" ("fastest viable training method at level 52... very expensive";
  mahogany tables then gnome benches, demon butler). "Levels 1-99: Mahogany Homes" cheaper alt.

=== AGILITY ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Agility_training
- "Levels 1-26/33: Questing" (The Tourist Trap -> 26). "Levels 20-47: Brimhaven Agility Arena" (floor spikes
  trap from lvl 20, tag pillars). "Levels 47-62: Wilderness Agility Course" ("fastest experience rates from
  level 47 to 62", deep Wilderness/PKers). "Levels 62-99: Hallowed Sepulchre" (needs Sins of the Father).
  "Levels 1-99: Rooftop Agility Courses" (marks of grace / graceful outfit).

=== HERBLORE ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Herblore_training
- "==Making potions==": "The most efficient way to gain Herblore experience is to make potions... The fastest
  Herblore experience is gained by simply making potions that offer the highest experience per hour."
- Potion level ladder from the table (level -> potion): 3 Attack, 12 Strength, 26 Energy, 38 Prayer,
  45 Super attack, 52 Super energy, 55 Super strength, 63 Super restore, 72 Ranging, 81 Saradomin brew,
  90 Super combat. Amulet of chemistry / prescription goggles noted as cost savers.

=== THIEVING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Thieving_training

- "Levels 45-49, 65-84/91/99: Blackjacking" (Pollnivneach, needs partial The Feud; rogue equipment, Sara brews;
  Bearded bandits 45-55, Pollnivnian bandits 55-65, Menaphite Thugs 65+).
- "Levels 49-65/99: Stealing artefacts" (Port Piscarilius). "Levels 91-99: Pyramid Plunder".

=== CRAFTING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Crafting_training
- "Levels 1-20: Leather items" (highest leather item, needle+thread). "Levels 20-62/77/99: Cutting gems"
  ("fastest experience up to level 77"; keep cutting diamonds to 62). "Levels 54-77/99: Battlestaves".
  "Levels 63/77-99: D'hide bodies".

=== FLETCHING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Fletching_training
- "Zero time methods" - Darts: "arguably the most feasible option for zero-time training" (amethyst/mithril/
  adamant). "Level 1-5/10: Arrow shafts". "Level 5-99: Bows" (cut & string; unstrung more AFK, stringing faster).

=== SLAYER ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_training
- "Access to Slayer masters": "obtain tasks from the highest-level Slayer master that your Combat and Slayer
  levels allow." Master gates: Turael/Spria any level; Mazchna 20 cb (Canifis); Vannaka 40 cb (Edgeville
  Dungeon); Chaeldar 70 cb (Zanaris, Lost City); Konar 75 cb; Nieve/Steve 85 cb (MM2); Duradel 100 cb+50
  Slayer (Shilo). Two-master switching for 10th/50th/100th/250th/1000th milestone points.

=== HUNTER ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Hunter_training
- "Levels 1-9: Natural History Quiz" (Varrock Museum, 1,000 xp). "Levels 9-44/80/99: Bird house trapping"
  ("fastest Hunter experience effectively up to level 80", Fossil Island, needs Bone Voyage, runs ~50 min;
  switch to drift net fishing at 44 if also training Fishing). "Levels 73-99: Black chinchompas" and
  "Levels 72/91-99: Hunters' Rumours" under Fastest active methods; "Levels 80-99: Herbiboar" (AFK).

=== MINING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Mining_training
- "Levels 1-15: Copper/Tin ore" (powermine/drop; Lumbridge Swamp). "Levels 15-45/70: Iron ore" (3-rock
  triangle spots; Varrock armour 10% double ore; Mining Guild best from 60). "Levels 45-99: Granite"
  (3-tick "3t4g" at Bandit Camp Quarry or Cape Conch mine = "fastest Mining experience from level 45";
  no tick-manip -> iron to 70 then Volcanic Mine). "Levels 30-99: Motherlode Mine" (low-effort AFK alt).

=== SMITHING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Smithing_training
- "Levels 1-39: Questing" (The Knight's Sword 12,725 xp etc.). "Levels 1-40: Anvil smithing" (highest item
  at west Varrock anvil). "Levels 40-99: Blast Furnace gold bars" ("fastest viable training method at level
  40", goldsmith gauntlets). "Levels 15-99: Giants' Foundry" alt.

=== FISHING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Fishing_training
- "Levels 1-24/33: Questing" (Sea Slug -> 24). "Levels 20-47/58: Fly fishing" (3-tick trout/salmon "fastest
  Fishing experience up to level 58"; static spot SE of Prifddinas; Tempoross faster if no tick-manip past 35).
  "Levels 47-99: Drift net fishing" (best for Fishing+Hunter together). "Levels 58-71/99: Barbarian Fishing"
  (3-tick, passive Agility & Strength). "Levels 71-99: 2-tick harpooning swordfish/tuna".

=== COOKING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Cooking_training
- "Levels 1-30: Poison karambwan" (1-tick, no reqs) and "Levels 1-30: Cooking fish" (sardine->salmon, cheaper).
- "Levels 30-99: Cooked karambwan" ("1-tick cooking karambwan provides the fastest Cooking experience", needs
  Tai Bwo Wannai Trio; Rogues' Den / Myths' Guild / Mor Ul Rek bankside; AFK -> Hosidius kitchen).

=== FIREMAKING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Firemaking_training
- "Levels 1-30: Creating pyre logs" (1-tick redwood/rosewood pyre logs, needs Shades of Mort'ton, fastest to 30).
- "Levels 1/30-99: Burning logs" ("one of the fastest ways to train Firemaking"; best at east Grand Exchange,
  bank 1 tile away; willow->teak->arctic pine->maple->mahogany->yew->magic->redwood). "Levels 50-99: Wintertodt".

=== WOODCUTTING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Pay-to-play_Woodcutting_training
- "Fastest experience": drop logs (powerchop). "Levels 1-15: Regular trees". "Levels 15-35: Oak trees"
  ("cutting oak trees offers slightly faster experience" than willows). "Levels 35-99: Teak trees"
  ("1.5-tick woodcutting teaks on Fossil Island is the fastest training method in the game"; no tick-manip ->
  teaks to 65 then sulliuscep).

=== FARMING ===
https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Farming_training
- "Levels 1-38: Questing" (skips low-level farming). "Levels 1-15: Bagged plants" (POH, "fastest way to train
  the early levels... significantly more expensive"). "Levels 15-99: Tree runs" ("the most effective way to
  gain Farming experience... by far the fastest effective experience"; plant highest tree/fruit tree/hardwood
  saplings, check health for XP, replant; herb runs for profit).
